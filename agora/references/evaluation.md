# Evaluation

IQ is never measured. Ágora measures observable evidence of performance. Translate labels and templates into the learner's language.

## 1. Metrics
| Metric | Calculation | Frequency | Indicative target [P] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recall without notes | correct / questions (partial = 0.5) | Every session; weekly mean | ≥85 % |
| Problems solved | Valid solutions per level (SKILL.md §7). Points = sum of the levels of valid solutions | Weekly | Rising in L3–L4 |
| Time to a valid solution | Median minutes per level | Weekly | Falling at equal level |
| Explanation quality | Rubric 0–4 | ≥2 per week | ≥3 |
| Essays and reports | Rubric 0–4 | Weekly | ≥3 from W8 |
| Transfer | Rubric 0–4 on L4 problems | Weekly | ≥3 |
| Calibration | \|prediction − result\| in percentage points; the sign shows over- or under-confidence | Every session | ≤10 |
| Quality of own questions | Rubric 0–4; mean of the week's best 3 | Weekly | ≥3 |
| Consistency | Sessions done / planned (in attention mode, report comebacks and sessions done instead) | Weekly | ≥80 % |
| Bouncing back | Days to resume after a missed session; missed criterion → met; comebacks (sessions after a gap of 2+ days) | Monthly | ≤1 day; ≤2 weeks |
| Repeated errors | % of the week's errors already in the log | Weekly | Falling |
| Long-term retention | Sample of 10 mature cards (stability ≥21 d or retired) | Monthly | ≥80 % |

## 2. Rubrics (0 absent · 1 emerging · 2 developing · 3 competent · 4 outstanding)
**Explanation** (mean of 4 criteria)
- Precision: 0 serious errors → 4 no errors, exact terms.
- Mechanism: 0 only describes → 2 names the cause without the chain → 4 complete causal chain from principles.
- Example and limits: 0 none → 4 own example plus the condition where it stops holding.
- Clarity for the audience: 0 undefined jargon → 4 a novice could reproduce it.

**Essay or report** (mean of 5 criteria; version 2 adds "revision")
- Thesis: debatable, precise, answers the question.
- Structure: every paragraph moves the argument forward.
- Evidence: relevant, high quality, cited and interpreted (not just quoted).
- Counter-argument: the strongest objection, not a straw man, and a reply.
- Precision of language: no ambiguity or padding.
- Revision (v2): changes respond to feedback and improve the argument.

**Problem** (each): 0 no setup · 1 partial setup · 2 correct setup with execution errors · 3 correct solution, or a reasonable and justified estimate · 4 correct and checked (units, edge cases, another route).

**Transfer:** 0 does not recognise the applicable principle · 1 recognises it with a hint · 2 applies it with errors · 3 applies it well · 4 applies it and explains why it holds, or what changes, in the new context.

**Own questions:** 0 none · 1 factual ("what is?") · 2 procedural ("how do you?") · 3 causal or conditional ("why?", "when does it fail?") · 4 generative or transfer ("what if…?", "what does it share with…?").

## 4. Monthly review (closing W4, W8, W12)
```
MONTHLY REVIEW — Weeks __–__
4-week trend per metric (↑ → ↓) and a one-sentence reading
Long-term retention: sample of 10 mature cards → __ %
Comparison with the diagnostic (W6 mini / W12 full): dimension by dimension
Consistency and bouncing back: …
Level: same / up / down (reason, with data)
Load: minutes/day for next month · is a deload week needed?
One method experiment for next month (e.g. more interleaving, retrieval at the end of the day)
```

## 5. Blockage diagnosis
Triggered by the same error type ≥3 times in 7 days, 2 sessions in a row below threshold, or a missed weekly criterion. Check in this order (causes can coexist):

| Cause | Signals | Quick test | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Fatigue | Sleep <6 h, energy ≤2, slips that increase towards the end, failing what was previously mastered | Compare the first and last third of the session | 30-min review-only session; rest rule; no new content until 2 nights of adequate sleep |
| 2. Missing prerequisites | Fails basic sub-steps; the error persists with H3 | 3 prerequisite questions | If fewer than 2/3 correct: a prerequisite mini-cycle (2–4 sessions) before moving on |
| 3. Poor strategy | Rereads, looks at solutions early, doesn't self-explain, attempts <5 min | Check how they studied in the log | Retrieval, a worked example with self-explanation, and the minimum attempt before any hint |
| 4. Lack of feedback | Repeats the same error with high confidence; doesn't know why it failed | Ask them to explain the correction in their own words | Line-by-line comparison with the model solution; a card for the error; a dedicated tutoring session |
| 5. Excessive difficulty | Gets L1–L2 right and fails L3+, even rested and with prerequisites | A bridging problem in between | Drop a level, bridging problems, move back up after 2 valid solutions |
