# Capstone projects (W11–W12)

All follow the same sequence: **question → research → reasoning → product v1 → feedback (Claude and, if possible, a real person) → v2 → defence**. Adapt the topic to the anchor subject and the learner's context and language.

| Area | Project | Research | Reason | Produce | Defend | Revise |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| STEM | A measurable question with own or public data (e.g. how a mug's cooling changes with its material, or an analysis of an open dataset) | Theoretical model and prior measurements | Hypothesis with a quantitative prediction; sources of error | Experiment or analysis, code or spreadsheet, and a lab-style report | 20-min defence on experimental error and alternative assumptions | v2 with better control or more data |
| Humanities / social sciences | Research essay on a disputed question, using primary sources | Historiography or state of the debate; ≥2 positions | Weigh the evidence for each position; own thesis | Essay of 2000–3000 words with notes | 45-min supervision and a written reply to the strongest objection | v2 integrating the reply |
| Business / decisions | Recommendation memo on a real decision (from the learner's organisation or a documented public case) | Market or organisational data; alternatives | Decision tree, expected value, sensitivity and pre-mortem | 2-page memo and a quantitative annex | Simulated committee: Claude plays a sceptical CFO, a customer and a regulator | v2 with the critical assumptions strengthened |
| Creativity / design | A design cycle for a real user | Interviews with 5 users; existing solutions | Define the problem; 20 ideas; explicit selection criteria | Prototype tested with 5 users | Present decisions and discards with evidence from the tests | 2 documented iterations |
| Languages | A real-world piece in the target language (podcast episode, presentation, interview, article) | Authentic models of the genre; audience | Plan structure, register and key vocabulary | Recording or text plus its corrected version | Live Q&A in the target language with unexpected follow-ups | v2 with errors per 100 words reduced |

**Project rubric (0–4 per criterion):** question (relevant and precise) · research (quality of sources or data) · reasoning (logic, alternatives considered) · product (quality, works) · defence (answers objections with evidence) · iteration (changes documented from feedback). Pass: mean ≥3 and no criterion <2.
